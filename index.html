Why I do both

By day I build AI systems at Mindvalley, and I have been facilitating for four years.

A while ago I organised a weekend for 35 people, and everything that I was doing was manual. I wrote the scripts, recorded, edited and posted, I sent a copied message on WhatsApp and shared my UPI ID, and I took a note of everyone in Notes. It was about 100 hours of work across three weeks, with late nights, and my body felt irritated and angry.

Then one weekend I was sitting in my living room on this yellow couch that I usually sit on, and I shared a voice note, and when I read what came back I realized that it sounded like me. My body felt relaxed. So now I do both: I work with breath and ice, and I build what lets a woman put some of that weight down.

I share the breath, the ice and what I am building as I go, on Instagram at @connectedtorhythm.
